LanguWay English › Course levels

English course: 30 levels, A1 to C1

LanguWay English is a free English course. It has 30 levels, 80 units and 240 lessons. Every lesson trains vocabulary, reading, writing, listening and speaking, and every unit ends with a grammar rule and a test. Start with the placement test to find your level.
